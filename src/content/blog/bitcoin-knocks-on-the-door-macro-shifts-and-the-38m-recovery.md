## The Macro Pivot is Real

The latest jobs report was the catalyst, but the underlying tension has been building for months. Inflation data has been sticky, keeping the Fed on hold. Today’s miss changed the calculus. Traders are no longer pricing in a "higher for longer" scenario; they are pricing in a soft landing, or worse, a hard landing that forces aggressive easing.

This shift matters for every asset class. When the dollar weakens, commodities rise. When yields fall, growth stocks and crypto outperform. We are seeing the early stages of a broad risk-on rotation. But here is the catch: everyone knows this trade now. The consensus is crowded. When the trade is crowded, the move gets violent.

We’ve seen this before. The last time the market priced in a pivot, BTC rallied hard then dumped 20% in a week as liquidity dried up temporarily. The lesson is simple: don’t fade the trend, but don’t chase the peak. The setup is there, but the risk is asymmetric if you’re overleveraged.
