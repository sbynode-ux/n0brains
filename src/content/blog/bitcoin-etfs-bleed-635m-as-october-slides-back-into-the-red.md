This is a clear signal that the current price level is unsupported by sustained institutional demand. Without fresh capital entering these vehicles, upside is capped by the sheer weight of existing positions. Traders who bought the dip expecting a smooth resumption of the bull trend are now holding the bag.

## Stock Market Contagion

The driver is not inside crypto. It is in the broader market. Fears around the AI trade are stoking fresh weakness in US equities. Investors are rotating out of high-valuation tech stocks and into cash or defensive assets.

Bitcoin, heavily weighted in the portfolios of large asset managers, is caught in this rotation. The move is mechanical. Risk parity models dictate that when volatility spikes in equities, crypto must be sold to maintain portfolio variance targets. This is not about Bitcoin fundamentals. It is about portfolio math.

The link between the two markets is now a direct pipeline for liquidity drain. Every negative headline in the S&P 500 translates to immediate outflows in the USDT or BTC ETFs. The decoupling trade is over. It never really existed.
