**40.6 million transactions per second.**

That is not a simulation. That is a live, on-chain stress test conducted by [Sui’s programmable offchain tunnels](https://cointelegraph.com/news/sui-offchain-network-hits-40m-tps-in-live-ai-focused-stress-test). The network didn’t just handle the load; it tunneled the data through AI agents to execute trades and state updates in real time. The infrastructure for autonomous market agents is no longer theoretical. It is benchmarked.

For traders and builders, this breaks the old mental model of blockchain throughput. We used to think of crypto as a settlement layer—slow, expensive, and batched. Sui is proving it can be a high-velocity execution layer for AI-driven capital. When agents can process millions of interactions per second, the latency between market signal and trade execution collapses to near zero.

## The Death of Manual Latency

The bottleneck in algorithmic trading has always been the gap between decision and execution. Even with co-located servers, there is slippage. There is network lag. There is the human or script overhead of checking conditions, signing transactions, and broadcasting.

Sui’s test shows that AI agents can act as the primary interface to the blockchain. They don’t just read data; they interpret it, negotiate terms, and execute complex multi-step trades across programmable tunnels. This is the difference between a passive bot and an active agent.

Passive bots react to price. Active agents react to context. They can analyze liquidity depth, adjust for slippage, and hedge positions across correlated assets before a human trader has finished loading their chart. The 40.6M TPS figure isn’t just a speed record. It is proof that the network can support the complexity of autonomous agents without fragmenting liquidity or freezing the chain.

## The Rise of Tokenized Execution

This isn’t just about crypto-native tokens. The same infrastructure that handles 40 million TPS can tokenize equities, indices, and commodities on platforms like Hyperliquid. When you combine this throughput with [on-chain liquidation maps](https://cointelegraph.com/news/sui-offchain-network-hits-40m-tps-in-live-ai-focused-stress-test), you get a trading environment that is far more transparent than traditional markets.

In traditional finance, you don’t see the liquidation cascades until they happen. In tokenized markets, you can see the heat zones. You can see where the leverage is stacked. You can see the exact distance between price and liquidation for thousands of positions.

Agents will use this data to hunt liquidity. They will push prices toward these zones to trigger stops and fill their own orders. If you are trading without visibility into this map, you are the liquidity.
