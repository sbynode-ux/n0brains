## The Mechanics of the Squeeze

Short squeezes are not about belief. They are about math. When bears pile into short positions, they leave behind a dense zone of potential liquidation. A small upward move triggers stop losses. Those forced buys push the price higher, triggering more stops. It is a feedback loop.

The recent move saw Bitcoin reclaim the **$80,000** level with force. This level has acted as both support and resistance for months. Breaking it with volume signals institutional interest. Breaking it on panic signals exhaustion.

The current setup suggests the latter is still a risk. The move was sharp, but the follow-through has been choppy. Traders who entered at the top are already underwater. Traders who held through the volatility are sitting on paper profits that evaporate quickly if momentum stalls.
