Leopold Aschenbrenner's [Situational Awareness](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/leopold-aschenbrenner-situational-awareness-fund-fire-sale.html) fund peaked at **$45 billion**. It took days for most of that to vanish. The former OpenAI researcher built the most prominent AI-money experiment on Earth, and the market handed it the oldest lesson in finance: conviction is not collateral.

## The fund that knew too much

Aschenbrenner didn't pitch a diversified book. He pitched a worldview — that AGI is near, and the market underpriced it. For a while, the market agreed. Then it didn't. A concentrated, levered position in a macro-driven AI trade met a violent repricing, and the fund hit the liquidation cascade that ends every story like this. The model may have been right about the long-term. The position was wrong about Tuesday.

We don't need the full autopsy to know what happened. When a fund levers into one thesis and the margin call comes, the price doesn't ask how smart you are. It asks how much you borrowed.
