When core inflation rises while headline figures might appear stable, it indicates sticky services costs and persistent wage pressures. This is the exact environment where central banks hesitate.

For crypto and tokenized assets, this means the liquidity faucet is not just dripping; it has stopped. Assets that rely on cheap money to sustain valuations face immediate repricing risk. The correlation between the DXY (US Dollar Index) and Bitcoin is likely to strengthen as the dollar re-rises on hawkish Fed expectations.

## The December Meeting: Binary Risk

The December FOMC meeting is now the single most important event in the macro calendar. With inflation ticking up, the probability of a cut has plummeted. Markets are likely over-leveraged on the assumption of a cut, creating a crowded short side against the macro reality.

This is a textbook setup for a volatility squeeze. If the Fed signals a hold or a hike, the dollar strengthens, and leverage gets flushed. If they signal a cut despite the data, the dollar weakens, and the rally resumes. But the path of least resistance is uncertainty.

Before taking a trade on this binary event, you need to assess the positioning. Are you long on overcrowded longs? Are you short against a potential hawkish surprise? The risk/reward is asymmetric. Entering a trade now without checking the liquidation maps is gambling, not trading.
