## The Midterm Reset

The historical pattern is clear. In the 90-day window after midterms, the market enters a phase of policy confirmation and liquidity pruning. This is not a crash signal. It is a divergence signal.

## The Macro Pulse Shift

This divergence is driven by the Macro Pulse. Daily Bitcoin and Ethereum bias shifts are no longer dictated solely by Fed dot plots or CPI prints. They are driven by on-chain liquidity conditions and tokenized equity flows.

When the stock market sneezes, crypto used to catch it. Now, crypto often stays under the blanket. This is because the liquidity sources are different. Equity markets are driven by institutional index rebalancing and ETF flows. Crypto markets are driven by stablecoin issuance, miner selling pressure, and DeFi yield cycles.

These two pools of money are no longer perfectly synchronized. A move in NVDA or the S&P 500 no longer automatically triggers a BTC buy order. The spillover is weaker. It is fragmented. It is noisy.
