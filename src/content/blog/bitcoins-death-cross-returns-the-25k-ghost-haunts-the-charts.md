## The Math of Momentum Failure

Moving averages are lagging indicators, but they are also self-fulfilling prophecies. When the short-term trend (21-day) breaks below the medium-term trend (150-day), it signals that recent buyers are underwater and that the medium-term buyers are finally capitulating.

In 2023, this crossover triggered a cascade of long liquidations. The market didn’t just drop; it unwound. Leverage that had been built up during the consolidation phase was swept out, creating a vacuum that pulled price down to test liquidity pools near **$25,000**.

Today, the macro environment is different, but the mechanics of leverage are not. If you are trading this move without understanding the liquidation heatmaps, you are gambling. The death cross is a warning that volatility is about to expand in the downside direction.

This creates the sharp wicks and deep draws that liquidate over-leveraged positions.
