Bitcoin just closed a weekly candle below its **200-week moving average**, a technical breakdown that historically signals the end of a macro bull regime. This isn't a minor dip. When a primary trend line of this magnitude fails, the market is no longer just correcting; it is re-pricing for a new, lower reality.

## The death of the long-term trend

The [200-week moving average](https://cointelegraph.com/markets/btc-price-loses-200-week-trend-line-as-2022-repeats?utm_source=rss_feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=rss_partner_inbound) has served as the ultimate floor for Bitcoin throughout the recent cycle. Losing this support means the medium-term momentum has officially surrendered to the bears. We are seeing a repeat of the structural weakness that defined the previous bear market, where every rally was met with aggressive selling as liquidity vanished. This breakdown suggests that the "buy the dip" mentality is currently fighting a losing battle against a shifting macro backdrop.

## Liquidity and the corporate pivot

While technicals are breaking, corporate behavior is shifting in the opposite direction. MicroStrategy recently [raised $334M through stock sales](https://cointelegraph.com/news/strategy-stock-sales-buys-no-bitcoin?utm_source=rss_feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=rss_partner_inbound), but they didn't put a single dollar into Bitcoin. Instead, they used the proceeds for dividends and repurchases, while parking **$149.1 million** in US dollars. This is a massive signal. When the largest corporate holder of Bitcoin chooses to build a cash pile and reward shareholders rather than accumulate more sats, it signals a defensive posture. The era of blind, aggressive accumulation by proxy is hitting a wall of capital preservation.
