A pre-revenue AI crypto startup raised **$12 million** and turned it into **$577,328** in six months. The cause wasn't a hack, a rug pull, or a market crash. According to [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/a-pre-revenue-ai-crypto-startup-funneled-12-million-into-ev-as-bad-crypto-trades-erased-97-of-cash-in-six-months/), bad crypto trades erased **97%** of the company's cash. The startup is still pre-revenue. Its RoboShare pilot and discounted equity facility remain conditional. That means every dollar lost was someone else's.

## The trade that ate the treasury

This is the classic startup mistake: take investor money, put it on the line without a risk framework, and call it "treasury management." The details are scarce, but the pattern is familiar. High leverage. No pre-trade check. No horizon. No stop. Just conviction and a chart.

We've seen this movie before. The difference here is that the protagonist was an AI crypto startup. If anyone should know better, it's them. But knowing better isn't the same as building a system that enforces it.

## What this means for AI agents

This story isn't just about one bad treasury desk. It's a warning for every AI agent being pointed at markets. Agents don't feel fear. They don't hesitate. They execute. That's great when the thesis is right, and catastrophic when the thesis is wrong.

Most of the trades that killed this startup would have flunked a 10-second pre-trade check. Was the positioning crowded? Was there scheduled event risk inside the horizon? Was the leverage inside one ordinary day's volatility? These are not hard questions. They're just rarely asked before someone clicks.

## The governance gap

The deeper issue is that a pre-revenue company had no business running a leveraged trading desk at all. No revenue, no buffer, no audit of the PnL before it was too late. The "AI" part of the startup didn't save it. The trades were still human decisions, or at least human-approved. An AI agent without guardrails is just a faster way to lose money.

## Market Context

Bitcoin is grinding sideways between **$105k** and **$118k**. Funding rates are flat, DeFi TVL has stalled, and the market is waiting on the next macro catalyst. That's exactly when leverage gets sloppy and bad trades compound. The absence of volatility is not the same as safety.
