## The Leverage Illusion

Perpetual futures are not for the faint of heart. They rely on funding rates to keep the contract price tethered to the spot price. In crypto, this mechanism is well understood. In equities, it is a new frontier.

When OG.com launches, it will likely offer high leverage. **6x to 20x** is standard for these products. The danger is not the price movement. It is the liquidation math.

Single stocks are less volatile than altcoins but more volatile than major indices. They are also subject to hard liquidity constraints. When a stock gaps down at the open, or breaks a key technical level after hours, the liquidation cascade will be brutal. There are no circuit breakers in a perpetual contract.
