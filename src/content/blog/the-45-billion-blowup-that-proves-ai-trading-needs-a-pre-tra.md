In days, [Leopold Aschenbrenner's](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/leopold-aschenbrenner-situational-awareness-fund-fire-sale.html) AI-focused hedge fund went from **$45 billion** under management to a fraction of that. The former OpenAI researcher built Situational Awareness into the fastest-growing fund on record with a simple thesis: AI eats the world, so buy the infrastructure. Then the market flinched. According to CNBC, most of the money evaporated inside a week.

The easy takeaway is "AI trading doesn't work." That's lazy and wrong. Directional edge was never the issue. The fund was levered, concentrated, and built for a world where the AI trade only goes up. When the path turned violent, no pre-trade check was in place to say "this setup fails inside one ordinary volatility event."

We keep skipping this step. We train models, backtest signals, deploy agents — then act surprised when a crowded, leveraged bet unwinds. The market doesn't care about your conviction. It cares about your liquidation distance.
