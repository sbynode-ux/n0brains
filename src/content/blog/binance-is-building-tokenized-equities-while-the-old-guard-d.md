## The risk of tokenized equities

Tokenized stocks are not just digital versions of traditional shares. They trade 24/7. They are leveraged. They are subject to on-chain liquidations.

When the stock market closes, tokenized equities on [Hyperliquid](https://www.hyperliquid.xyz/) keep moving. This creates unique risks. A gap down in Friday’s close can wipe out a position before the market opens on Monday.
