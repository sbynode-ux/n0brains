## The Buyback Trap

Hyperliquid’s rise has been fueled by a flywheel: high trading volume generates revenue, which funds HYPE buybacks, which reduces supply, which pushes the price up. This works until it doesn’t. The model relies on consistent, high-margin trading activity.

Binance’s involvement changes the math. As a major liquidity provider and partner, Binance extracts value from the ecosystem. That extraction directly reduces the pool of funds available for HYPE buybacks. The token’s price was never just a reflection of Hyperliquid’s success. It was a reflection of Hyperliquid’s *net* success after accounting for partner costs.

When Binance takes its cut, the buyback engine slows. The price drop isn’t a market correction. It’s a mechanical adjustment. Traders who didn’t model this dependency are now holding bags. The lesson is clear: always check the revenue flow before buying the token.
