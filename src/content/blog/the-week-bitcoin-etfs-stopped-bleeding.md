The recovery wasn’t driven by a moonshot in price or a sudden regulatory green light. It was driven by exhaustion. Traders who had rotated out of crypto during the October dip were forced back in when the slide stalled.

The market is not healing. It is just pausing.

The rest of the pack—Fidelity, Grayscale, and the smaller issuers—were largely along for the ride. ## Regulatory Fragmentation

While flows stabilize in the US, regulatory clarity is fracturing elsewhere. South Korea is moving to amend its forex law to regulate stablecoins as payment instruments. In Hong Kong, HSBC launched its first HKD stablecoin, RedCoin, to retail users. In Southeast Asia, Singapore, Malaysia, and Thailand are expanding their BLOOM initiative for cross-border stablecoin settlement.

This fragmentation creates arbitrage opportunities. It also creates risk. A stablecoin issued in one jurisdiction may not be honored in another during a stress event. Cross-border flows are increasing, but the legal frameworks backing them are still diverging.
