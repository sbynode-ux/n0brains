## The After-Hours Arbitrage

Traditional market participants operate on a schedule. Open at 9:30 AM ET. Close at 4:00 PM ET. After-hours trading exists, but it is thin, expensive, and fraught with execution risk.

On-chain tokenized equities ignore the bell.

The volume on Solana and BNB Chain suggests that traders are using these platforms for two distinct behaviors. First, they are hedging overnight gap risk. If you hold a long position in a US-listed tech stock, you are exposed to news events that break while the market sleeps. Tokenized equities allow you to offset that exposure instantly, without waiting for the next morning’s open.

Second, they are speculating on pre-market sentiment. When earnings drop at 4:05 PM ET, the price discovery happens on-chain before the cash market even reacts to the headlines. The on-chain price leads. The cash market follows.

This creates a dangerous asymmetry for retail traders who assume tokenized stocks behave like their underlying assets. They don’t. They have different liquidity pools, different maker-taker dynamics, and different liquidation triggers.
