## The physical AI breakout

For years, the market has treated AI as a software play. Large language models, chatbots, and coding assistants have driven the initial wave of capital. But the narrative is shifting from digital intelligence to embodied intelligence. Unitree is at the epicenter of this transition. The **629%** increase suggests that investors are no longer satisfied with software that just writes emails; they want software that moves through the world.

This surge reflects a broader trend where robotics companies are being revalued as AI infrastructure plays. We are seeing a transition from "AI as a service" to "AI as a physical agent." This shift creates massive volatility in the tech sector, as capital rotates rapidly between pure-play software firms and companies capable of hardware-software integration.
