## The Mechanics of the Reclaim

The current setup mirrors previous cycle bottoms. In 2020 and 2023, Bitcoin dipped below short-term moving averages to touch or slightly breach long-term trend lines before reversing sharply. The 20-month line represents the average cost basis of long-term holders over nearly two years. It is the floor where institutional accumulation typically accelerates.

CryptoQuant’s data indicates that Bitcoin’s market cycle structure remains bullish, but it requires this specific technical maneuver. The price action today is less about sentiment and more about structural hygiene. The market is clearing out high-leverage longs that entered during the recent euphoria.

This is not a crash. It is a calibration.

The implication for traders is straightforward. Buying the breakout above resistance is easy. Buying the fear near the trend line is hard. That is where the asymmetric risk-reward exists. The trend line holds because it is backed by long-term supply that has not moved in years. It is the most statistically significant support level in the asset’s history.
