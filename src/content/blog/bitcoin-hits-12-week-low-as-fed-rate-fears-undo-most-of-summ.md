### The Macro Squeeze

The core problem is simple: higher rates for longer. Inflation remains sticky, and the market has priced in a scenario where the central bank tightens rather than eases. This creates a vacuum for risk assets.

Crypto has become a leveraged bet on global liquidity expansion. When that expansion stalls, the leverage unwinds violently. We are seeing this play out in real-time. The summer rally was built on the expectation of cheap money. That expectation has evaporated.

Traders who bought the dip in September are now underwater. The "buy the rumor, sell the news" cycle has inverted into "buy the rumor, get liquidated." The market is punishing anyone who mistook a monetary policy pause for a pivot to easing.
