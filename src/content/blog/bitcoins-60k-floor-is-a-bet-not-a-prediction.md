"Bitcoin will never fall below $60K again."

That is not a forecast. That is a bet — a confident one from [Nansen founder Alex Svanevik](https://cointelegraph.com/magazine/bitcoin-will-never-fall-below-60k-again-nansen-founder?utm_source=rss_feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=rss_partner_inbound). His logic: crypto is growing up as real-world assets come on-chain. He's right that the infrastructure is maturing. But the market doesn't reward confident bets. It rewards priced risk. And a "never" floor is the most mispriced risk in crypto right now.

## The floor is a liquidity event, not a narrative

The bullish case for a $60K floor is real. The [IMF says domestic stablecoins could boost demand for dollar-backed tokens](https://cointelegraph.com/news/imf-domestic-stablecoins-dollar-token-demand?utm_source=rss_feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=rss_partner_inbound), and Circle just expanded native USDC to OKX's [X Layer](https://cointelegraph.com/news/circle-native-usdc-okx-x-layer-cctp?utm_source=rss_feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=rss_partner_inbound). The dollar's on-ramp into crypto is getting denser. Tokenized equities, stablecoin reserves, institutional custody — the order book is deeper than it was in 2021.

But a floor is not a price level where people *believe*. A floor is a price level where buy orders are deep enough to absorb a cascade. That depth is only there when spot buyers show up with no leverage, when funding is negative, when open interest is thin below the level. None of that is permanent. The "never below $40K" crowd of 2021 learned that lesson after the market spent 2022 grinding them out. A floor is a liquidity event, not a narrative.

## The floor can be measured

The good news: we can measure the actual floor. At $60K, what is the open interest? What is the funding rate? How much liquidation is stacked below? If the floor is real, then $60K is a liquidation magnet, not a story. If the floor is a story, then the order book below it is empty.

## The floor is now crowded

The bigger problem: the "never below $60K" line is already consensus. Every trader has heard it. Every dip buyer is positioned for it. That means the floor is crowded. When a level becomes famous, it becomes the place where the exit door is narrow. The moment a macro print or a liquidation cascade hits, the crowd will be selling the floor, not buying it.

## Market Context

Bitcoin trades well above $60K, and the funding picture looks comfortable. Rates are positive, open interest is climbing, and the RWA maturity thesis is everywhere. DeFi TVL is flat, while tokenized equities on Hyperliquid are slowly building. The sentiment is calm — which is exactly when a "permanent floor" gets tested.

## The signal

The signal is not the $60K claim. The signal is the structural shift behind it. Real-world assets are making crypto more institutional, which means the risk is no longer just a single exchange or a hack. The risk is a spillover: when the stock market sneezes, crypto catches it. The cross-asset map matters more than the floor.

## The takeaway

"Never" belongs in a headline, not in an order book. The floor is not a promise. It's a trade. Check it.
