This created a feedback loop that lasted until shorts were forced to buy back positions to cover their losses.

The speed of this reversal highlights the fragility of current market structure. Traders who held spot during the dip made money. Traders who shorted the top got wrecked. Traders who tried to catch the falling knife without risk management got liquidated twice.
