The core issue is liquidity concentration. Strategy’s market cap is heavily correlated to its Bitcoin holdings. When you remove the index inclusion premium, you remove a significant chunk of the bid support.

1.  **Passive funds sell:** ETFs and index funds must divest.
2.  **Active funds follow:** Momentum traders see the structural break and short the weakness.
3.  **Liquidity dries up:** With fewer buyers, the bid-ask spread widens, causing slippage to amplify the price drop.
