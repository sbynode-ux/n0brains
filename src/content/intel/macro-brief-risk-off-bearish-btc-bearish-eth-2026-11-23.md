The market is currently anchored in a regime of **Extreme Fear**, with the Fear & Greed Index holding steady at **11-12** for seven consecutive days. This is not a healthy consolidation; it is a compression of liquidity and confidence that typically precedes sharp directional moves rather than sustained sideways chop. While the absence of high-impact USD data releases over the past week has kept volatility suppressed, the underlying sentiment is structurally bearish, favoring short-side opportunities or defensive hedging over long entries.

## The Setup — What Released (And What Didn't)

The defining feature of this week’s macro landscape is the silence. There were **zero** USD high-impact events released in the covered window. Usually, a void in scheduled data allows trends to drift, but here it has acted as a pressure cooker. The lack of fresh information has forced traders to rely entirely on technical positioning and sentiment indicators, both of which are currently screaming caution.

This quiet period is deceptive. It masks the accumulation of risk rather than dissipating it. When the market is this fearful and data-quiet, the next catalyst carries disproportionate weight. The upcoming **Core PCE Price Index** and **Prelim GDP** prints on **November 25** will not just be data points; they will be the trigger that breaks the current equilibrium. The market is not ignoring these events; it is waiting for them to justify a direction. Until then, the bias remains tilted toward the downside, driven by the sheer weight of pessimistic positioning.

## BTC — Directional Bias, Conviction, Key Levels

**Bitcoin** is trading at **$61,371**, a level that reflects a market struggling to find buyers at higher valuations. The **24-hour change** of **+0.59%** is negligible, indicating a complete lack of conviction in the upside. The directional bias is **Bearish**.

The conviction level is **High**. The seven-day streak of **Extreme Fear** readings (**10-12**) is a lagging but reliable indicator of capitulation pressure. When sentiment stays this low for this long, it often signals that weak hands have already folded, but it also means there is very little buying interest to absorb any selling pressure. The market is fragile.

Key levels to watch are the **$60,000** psychological support and the **$62,500** resistance. A break below **$60,000** would likely accelerate selling as stop-losses cluster there. Conversely, a move above **$62,500** would require significant volume to sustain, which is currently absent. The invalidation of this bearish call would be a sudden surge in spot ETF inflows or a positive GDP print that suggests a "soft landing" is already priced in and now being rewarded.

## ETH — Directional Bias, Conviction, Key Levels

**Ethereum** is trading at **$1,724.06**, showing slightly more resilience than BTC with a **+1.01%** daily gain, but the broader trend remains aligned with the risk-off regime. The directional bias is **Bearish**.

The conviction level is **Medium-High**. While ETH has outperformed BTC in the last 24 hours, this is likely a temporary rotation rather than a fundamental shift. The **Extreme Fear** sentiment affects all risk assets, and ETH is no exception. The lack of positive catalysts in the DeFi or L2 space means there is no internal momentum to counter the external macro pressure.

Key levels are **$1,700** support and **$1,800** resistance. A break below **$1,700** would open the door to **$1,650**, a level that has acted as a floor in previous cycles. A move above **$1,800** would require a breakout in BTC to lead the way, which is currently not on the table. The invalidation of this bearish call would be a sharp decline in the DXY (US Dollar Index) or a surprise hawkish pivot from the Fed that de-risks the equity markets, providing a tailwind for altcoins.
