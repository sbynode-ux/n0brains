The market is currently sitting at a dangerous inflection point: sentiment is stuck in the **70-74** Greed zone, but price action has failed to make new highs, signaling that the bulls are exhausted. We are looking at a classic distribution phase where retail FOMO meets institutional distribution, creating a high-probability short setup for the week ahead.

## The Setup

The most recent data releases from last week were the fuel for this fatigue. The **Non-Farm Employment Change** came in at **89K**, missing the forecast of **89K** and crashing well below the previous month’s **162K**. While the **Unemployment Rate** held steady at **4.1%**, the labor market is clearly cooling faster than the Fed would like. This dovish disappointment usually supports risk assets initially, but the initial pump has already happened.

Simultaneously, inflation remains sticky. The **Core PCE Price Index m/m** came in hotter than expected at **0.3%** versus the forecast of **0.2%**, and the **Final GDP q/q** held at **1.5%**. The combination of weak labor and sticky inflation is a nightmare for the Federal Reserve. They cannot cut aggressively without reigniting inflation, but they cannot hold rates too high without breaking the labor market. This policy paralysis is the primary driver of the current market stagnation.

The **Fear & Greed Index** has hovered near **74** for the past three days. In a healthy uptrend, greed should be accompanied by higher highs. Here, we see lower highs on BTC and ETH while greed remains elevated. This divergence is the warning sign. When sentiment is this high but price refuses to break out, a mean reversion to the downside is statistically more likely than a continuation.

## BTC — directional bias, conviction, key levels to watch, what invalidates the call

**Bitcoin** is currently trading at **$84,627**, down **1.92%** in the last 24 hours. The bias is bearish for the short term. The key level to watch is the **$85,000** psychological resistance. Every time price approaches this zone, selling pressure intensifies. The invalidation of our bearish call is a clean, high-volume close above **$86,500**. Without that breakout, any rally into resistance is a sell signal. We expect price to test support around **$82,000** as leverage is flushed out.

## ETH — same structure as BTC

**Ethereum** is underperforming Bitcoin, trading at **$2,684.28**, down **2.14%** in the last 24 hours. The ETH/BTC pair is weakening, indicating that capital is rotating out of altcoins back into stablecoins or Bitcoin. The bias is bearish. The key level is **$2,750**. If ETH fails to hold this level, we expect a drop to **$2,600**. Invalidation requires a strong break above **$2,850** with volume. Currently, there is no sign of such strength.
