## The Setup

When sentiment is this stretched against a hard macro backdrop, the correction is not a matter of if, but when. ## BTC — Bearish Bias

Bitcoin is facing headwinds from both macro policy and internal structure. The **FOMC** minutes confirmed that the central bank is not ready to cut rates. Higher-for-longer rates strengthen the USD, which is inversely correlated to **BTC** performance.

The bullish thesis relies on ETF inflows or a breakout above key resistance. Neither is present. The price action shows lower highs on the daily chart. The **Greed** sentiment suggests overcrowding. When everyone is long, there is no one left to buy.

**Key Level:** Watch **$80,000**. A break below this psychological support with volume confirms the bearish shift.
**Invalidation:** A daily close above **$85,000** would invalidate this call and suggest the hawkish minutes were already priced in.

## ETH — Bearish Bias

Ethereum is lagging Bitcoin. The **ETH/BTC** pair is weakening. This is typical in risk-off environments where capital rotates out of higher-beta assets. The **Greed** sentiment in crypto does not necessarily mean bullishness for every altcoin. It often means leverage is stacked.

**$2,500** is the support level. If **BTC** breaks down, **ETH** will fall faster. The tokenized equity markets on Hyperliquid reflect this risk aversion. Stock-market liquidation maps show tighter buffers for leveraged positions in tech-heavy tokens.

**Key Level:** **$2,450**. A break below this opens the door to **$2,200**.
**Invalidation:** A sustained move above **$2,700** with increasing volume would signal a rotation back into Ethereum-specific narratives.
