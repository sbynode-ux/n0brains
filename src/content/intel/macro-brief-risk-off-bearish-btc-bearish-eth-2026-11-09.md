Bitcoin is trading at **$74,366**, down **2.01%** in 24 hours, while Ethereum has bled harder at **$2,112.74**, shedding **5.59%** as traders flatten leverage ahead of the week’s inflation data.

We are in a risk-off regime. The lack of high-impact USD macro data this week has not provided relief; instead, it has allowed fear to compound. The absence of a catalyst has turned into a vacuum, and liquidity is draining out of the system.

## The Setup

The macro calendar for the past seven days was empty of USD high-impact events. Usually, this silence creates a consolidation phase. Here, it created a vacuum that fear filled. Without fresh headlines to distract market participants, the underlying weakness in crypto assets has become the primary narrative.

This is dangerous. When no news is good news, traders assume stability. When no news is no news, traders assume stagnation. In a downtrend, stagnation is just a pause before the next leg down. The market is currently waiting for the **Core CPI** and **CPI m/m** prints on Tuesday to dictate the next directional move. Until then, every bounce is likely to be sold into.

The sentiment data confirms this exhaustion. We have seen seven consecutive days of readings below **50**, with four days classified as "Fear" and three as "Extreme Fear." This is not a healthy correction. This is a lack of buyers.

## BTC — Directional Bias: Bearish

Bitcoin’s bias is bearish. The move below **$75,000** has broken short-term structural support. At **$74,366**, we are testing the lower bound of the current consolidation range. The daily close below this level would confirm a shift from consolidation to distribution.

The key level to watch is **$72,000**. This is the psychological support that has held during previous dips. If BTC fails to hold this level on a daily close, we expect a rapid flush toward **$70,000**.

Conviction is moderate-high. The risk is skewed to the downside because the macro backdrop is not supportive of risk assets. The invalidation of this call is a daily close above **$76,500**. Only a sustained move above this resistance level would suggest the bearish thesis is wrong and a reversal is underway. Until then, shorts are the higher probability play.

## ETH — Directional Bias: Bearish

Ethereum is underperforming Bitcoin, which is a classic sign of risk-off sentiment. ETH is trading at **$2,112.74**, down **5.59%** in 24 hours. The ETH/BTC pair is weakening, indicating that capital is rotating out of altcoins and into stablecoins or Bitcoin itself.

The key level for ETH is **$2,050**. This is the support zone that has held during recent volatility. If ETH breaks below **$2,050**, we expect a drop toward **$2,000**. The momentum is strongly negative, and there is no sign of buyer exhaustion.

Conviction is high. The correlation between ETH and macro risk assets is stronger than usual, and with CPI data looming, traders are avoiding leverage in ETH. The invalidation of this call is a daily close above **$2,250**. Until then, the path of least resistance is down.
