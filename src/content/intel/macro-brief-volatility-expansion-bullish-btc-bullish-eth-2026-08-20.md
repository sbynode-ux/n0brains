The **FOMC Meeting Minutes** released on **August 19** confirm that the era of aggressive tightening is officially dead, paving the way for a liquidity-driven mania.

## The Setup

The market spent the last week drowning in **Fear**, with the **Fear & Greed Index** bottoming out at **29** before a sudden, violent reversal. We saw a cluster of readings between **31** and **46** that suggested a capitulation event was underway. However, the **FOMC Meeting Minutes** acted as the catalyst for a massive regime shift. The minutes revealed a consensus among policymakers that inflation is sufficiently anchored, shifting the conversation from "how high" to "how fast" rates will be cut.

This is not a slow grind higher. We are seeing a liquidity injection signal. When the Fed pivots from restrictive to neutral, the first assets to catch the overflow are the high-beta proxies. The recent jump in **BTC** to **$71,900** and **ETH** to **$2,280** isn't just a relief rally; it is the market repricing the cost of capital in real-time. The sentiment flip from **29** to **62** in a single week proves that the downside was overextended and the liquidity vacuum is being filled.

## BTC

We are moving into a high-conviction **Bullish** regime for **Bitcoin**. The **11.65%** move in the last **24 hours** has cleared several psychological and technical hurdles. We expect **BTC** to test the **$75,000** zone within the next two weeks as momentum builds. The primary driver is the widening spread between real yields and crypto volatility. As yields soften, the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding assets like **BTC** collapses.

Our bias remains intact as long as we hold above the **$68,500** support level. This level represents the pivot point where the recent "fear" regime met the new "liquidity" regime. If **BTC** closes a daily candle below **$68,500**, the current breakout is a bull trap, and we revert to a range-bound outlook. Watch the **$72,000** level closely; a clean break above this on high volume validates the next leg toward **$80,000**.

## ETH

**Ethereum** is currently outperforming **Bitcoin** on a relative strength basis, posting an **18.84%** gain in the last **24 hours**. This suggests that capital is not just flowing into the market, but specifically into the ecosystem's utility layer. We are **Bullish** on **ETH**, targeting **$2,500** as the immediate milestone. The rotation into **ETH** often precedes a broader DeFi summer, and the current price action suggests the engine is warming up.

The key level to watch is **$2,150**. This was a major resistance zone that has now flipped to support. If **ETH** can consolidate above **$2,200**, the path to **$2,500** is wide open. The invalidation point for this call is a drop back below **$2,050**, which would suggest that the **ETH/BTC** pair is failing to capture the macro tailwinds. We are looking for a sustained period of high volume to confirm that this isn't just a leveraged squeeze, but a structural trend change.

## Calendar Risks

While the macro backdrop is favorable, two specific risks could flip this bullish thesis into a "fake-out" scenario. First, keep a close eye on any unexpected spikes in the **U.S. Dollar Index (DXY)**. A sudden strengthening of the dollar would suck liquidity out of risk assets and likely crush the current momentum. Second, watch for any sudden shifts in **Treasury Yield** volatility. If the market begins to price in a "hard landing" rather than a "soft landing," the pivot will be viewed as a desperation move rather than a growth catalyst, leading to a violent sell-off.
