The market is pricing in a higher-for-longer interest rate environment, and the data supports it.

The **FOMC Meeting Minutes** released on **November 18** confirmed that policymakers are not yet ready to cut. Simultaneously, the **Core Retail Sales** print for **November 17** came in at **0.4%**, significantly below the **0.9%** previous month. The combination of hawkish central bank signals and weakening consumer demand creates a clear risk-off regime. We are not looking for a reversal. We are looking for shorts.

## The Setup

The narrative has shifted from "when will the Fed cut?" to "how long can the economy sustain this?" The **FOMC Meeting Minutes** did not offer any dovish surprises. Instead, they reinforced the view that inflation remains sticky enough to warrant patience. This removes the primary tailwind for crypto assets, which have been rallying on the back of liquidity expectations.

Compounding this is the consumption data. **Retail Sales** growth slowed to **0.1%**, missing the **0.1%** forecast. **Core Retail Sales** decelerated to **0.4%** from **0.9%**. These numbers suggest that the consumer is pulling back. In a risk-off environment, capital flows out of speculative assets like **BTC** and **ETH** and into safer havens or cash. The **Fear & Greed Index** has stayed locked in **Extreme Fear** for nearly a week, hovering between **10** and **20**. This is not a temporary dip. It is a sustained sentiment shift.

The **Fed Chairman Warsh Speaks** on **November 16** did little to calm nerves. His comments were measured but firm, avoiding any language that suggests urgency. The market reacted with caution, and that caution is now hardening into a trend.

## BTC — directional bias

**Bitcoin** is trading at **$63,907**, down **0.41%** in the last 24 hours. The immediate bias is bearish. The lack of a catalyst to break the current resistance levels, combined with the macro headwinds, suggests further downside.

We expect **BTC** to test lower support levels. The key level to watch is **$60,000**. A break below this level would confirm the bearish momentum. The invalidation point for this call is any surprise dovish signal from the Fed or a stronger-than-expected economic data release. Until then, we assume the path of least resistance is down.

The **Extreme Fear** sentiment means that any small negative news will be amplified. Whales are likely accumulating, but retail is fleeing. This divergence creates volatility, but the direction remains clear. We do not chase longs here. We look for short entries on rallies.

## ETH — directional bias

**Ethereum** is trading at **$1,806.16**, up **0.37%** in the last 24 hours. The slight gain is noise. The underlying trend is bearish. **ETH** is heavily correlated with **BTC**, and the macro environment is not favorable for altcoins.

We expect **ETH** to underperform **BTC** in this risk-off regime. The key level to watch is **$1,750**. A break below this level would signal further weakness. The invalidation point is the same as **BTC**: a surprise dovish Fed pivot or a strong economic rebound.

The lack of upcoming high-impact USD events means the market is trading on momentum and sentiment. With **Extreme Fear** dominating, sentiment is the primary driver. We expect continued selling pressure on any bounce. The bias is to short the rallies.

## Calendar Risks

The week ahead has no scheduled USD high-impact events in the current covered window. This absence of data is a risk in itself. The market will trade on rumors and technicals. Any unexpected leak or commentary from Fed officials could flip the call.

The primary risk to our bearish view is a sudden shift in sentiment driven by external factors, such as a geopolitical event or a surprise regulatory announcement. However, given the current data, we remain focused on the macro headwinds. We do not bet against the Fed.
