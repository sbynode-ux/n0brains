Usually, this quiet period is where traders get complacent, building long positions on the assumption that the status quo will hold. They are wrong. The absence of data does not mean the absence of risk; it means the risk is accumulating in the shadows.

The market is now staring down a week of high-impact events that will force a repricing. The first crack appears tomorrow when Fed Chairman Warsh speaks. Central bank communication is no longer just about guidance; it is about damage control. In an extreme fear environment, any hawkish tilt is interpreted as a tightening squeeze.

This deceleration is a red flag. If consumer spending is stalling, earnings expectations will drop, and risk assets like crypto and tokenized equities will be the first to sell off. ## ETH

Ethereum is following Bitcoin but with higher beta. At **$1,807.08**, ETH is lagging in percentage terms but leading in pain. The correlation between BTC and ETH remains high, but ETH suffers from additional overhead from staking yields and token unlocks. In a risk-off environment, ETH underperforms because it is viewed as a tech stock proxy rather than a pure monetary asset.

The bias for ETH is bearish. The same levels that apply to BTC apply to ETH, but with a wider margin of error. ETH needs to hold **$1,750** to maintain any hope of a bounce. If it breaks, the drop to **$1,650** is likely. The sentiment data suggests that traders are overly leveraged on ETH longs, making it a prime target for liquidation cascades.

We expect ETH to continue to underperform BTC until the macro uncertainty clears. The risk-reward for longs is terrible. The fear index for ETH-specific assets is even deeper, indicating that the pain is not over.
