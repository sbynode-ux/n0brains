Bitcoin is holding a tight range near **$86,118** despite a week of zero high-impact USD macro data. The market is not confused; it is waiting. The **Fear & Greed Index** has remained anchored in **Greed** territory for seven straight days, averaging **71**, which suggests retail confidence is high while smart money is positioning for the **FOMC Meeting Minutes** release on **October 7**.

## The Setup

For the past **7** days, the macro calendar has been empty of USD high-impact prints. This silence is not benign. It is a vacuum that price action is currently filling with range-bound consolidation. BTC moved only **+0.03%** in the last **24** hours, and ETH slipped **-0.12%** to **$2,712.73**. This lack of directional velocity is typical before a major liquidity event.

The **FOMC Meeting Minutes** from the last Federal Reserve gathering are scheduled for release on **October 7** at **18:00 UTC**. These minutes will reveal the internal debate among policymakers. Are they worried about sticky inflation? Are they ready to cut rates? Or is there a split in the committee? The market has been pricing in a stable environment, but the minutes often contain the hidden tensions that cause volatility spikes.

We are seeing a divergence in asset behavior. BTC is defending its support levels with discipline, while ETH is lagging. This suggests that capital is rotating into the store-of-value narrative rather than speculative smart-contract exposure. The lack of recent data has allowed technical levels to become sharp again. When data returns, those levels will be tested aggressively.

## BTC

**Bitcoin** is exhibiting bullish consolidation. The asset is holding above key psychological support near **$85,000**. The daily range is compressing, which usually precedes an expansion move. Given the **Greed** sentiment, a short squeeze is a distinct possibility if the FOMC minutes are less hawkish than feared.

The directional bias is **Bullish** with moderate conviction. The invalidation level is a daily close below **$84,500**. If BTC breaks that floor, the consolidation pattern fails, and we could see a rapid flush to **$82,000** as leveraged longs get liquidated. However, the current price action shows buyers stepping in at the lower end of the range.

We are watching the **$86,000** to **$87,000** resistance zone. A break above this with volume would confirm the continuation of the uptrend. Until then, we are in a holding pattern. The key is to avoid over-leveraging into the **FOMC** release. The minutes can move markets faster than the initial CPI or NFP prints because they reveal future intent, not just past data.

## ETH

**Ethereum** is showing **Neutral** bias. It is underperforming BTC in this consolidation phase. The ratio is weakening, which indicates that liquidity is not flowing into altcoins as aggressively as it is into BTC. ETH is trading at **$2,712.73**, and the lack of catalysts is keeping it flat.

The support level is **$2,650**. A break below this would signal weakness and potential outflows from DeFi protocols. The resistance is **$2,800**. Without a specific ETH-centric catalyst, the price action will likely follow BTC’s lead but with lower magnitude.

The **Neutral** call is based on the lack of independent momentum. ETH needs BTC to break out decisively to gain traction. If BTC chops, ETH will likely bleed slowly against the pair. Traders should be cautious about long ETH positions until the macro uncertainty clears.

## Calendar Risks

The **FOMC Meeting Minutes** on **October 7** are the only high-impact USD event this week. The risk is not the direction of the minutes, but the divergence within the Fed. If the minutes show a split between hawks and doves, expect volatility in both BTC and ETH regardless of the net outcome.

Secondary risk comes from on-chain liquidation maps. High leverage in the current range means that a small move can trigger cascading liquidations. We are seeing elevated open interest in BTC futures, which increases the risk of a stop-run before the actual news release.
