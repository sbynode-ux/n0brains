The absence of data is a data point in itself. Markets hate uncertainty, but they hate complacency more. When the news flow is silent, the only thing left to move price is the unwinding of overextended positions. We are seeing a divergence between on-chain sentiment and off-chain macro reality.

## ETH — Bearish

Ethereum is trading at **$2,742.38**, up **0.99%** in the last 24 hours. ETH is underperforming BTC on a relative basis, indicating weak underlying demand. The directional bias is Bearish. We expect ETH to bleed against BTC as traders rotate into safer assets or exit entirely.

The key level to watch is **$2,600**. A break below this support will signal that ETH is losing its bid, likely triggering a cascade of leveraged long exits in the DeFi sector. The conviction is moderate-high. The lack of catalysts for ETH is more pronounced than for BTC, as there are no major network upgrades or ETF flows expected this week.

ETH is vulnerable to any spill-over from BTC weakness. If Bitcoin drops **5%**, Ethereum will likely drop **7-8%**. The beta is high, and the sentiment is fragile. We are seeing signs of distribution on the hourly charts, with higher highs being met with lower volumes. This is a classic sign of exhaustion.
