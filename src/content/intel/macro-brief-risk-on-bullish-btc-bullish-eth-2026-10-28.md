## The Setup

The past week was quiet. No high-impact USD data released that moved the needle. This silence is deceptive. It allows leverage to build up unchecked in the background. When the market is quiet, liquidity dries up, and price becomes thin.

Now, the calendar is exploding.

On **2026-10-28** at **18:00**, the **FOMC Statement** and **Federal Funds Rate** decision drop. The market expects **4.00%**. A hold is the baseline. If they hold, the initial reaction is usually a "buy the rumor, sell the news" event, or a brief pump followed by a drift lower as traders take profits. The real risk is the **FOMC Press Conference** at **18:30**. Powell’s tone will dictate the next week’s direction.

Then, just 24 hours later on **2026-10-29** at **12:30**, the **Advance GDP q/q** print arrives with a forecast of **3.2%** against a previous **1.5%**. This is a massive revision expectation. If the economy is actually growing at that pace, the Fed is less likely to cut soon. That would be hawkish for the USD and bearish for crypto. Conversely, if growth misses, the narrative shifts to easing, which pumps crypto.

The contradiction is the key. We have a rate hold (bullish) colliding with potential economic outperformance (hawkish/USD bullish). This tension creates the exact kind of whipsaw market that wipes out unprepared traders.

## BTC

**Bitcoin** is trading in a range, but the bias is **Bullish** heading into the week. The logic is simple: a rate hold removes the primary bearish catalyst for the last six months. Lower rates mean cheaper liquidity, and crypto is the most sensitive asset to liquidity shifts.

However, this bull case is heavily dependent on the **FOMC Press Conference** not introducing new inflation fears. If Powell hints that the **4.00%** rate is the new floor, risk assets will sell off.

Key level to watch: The previous weekly high. If **BTC** breaks above this with volume, the path is clear to the next resistance zone. Invalidating the call is a hawkish surprise in the **FOMC Statement** or a miss on the **Advance GDP** that triggers a USD surge. If the **DXY** spikes, **BTC** will follow it down, regardless of the rate decision.

We are long, but we are not blind. The crowd is buying the rate hold. We are buying the rate hold *if* the press conference is dovish. If it’s neutral, we stay flat. If it’s hawkish, we short.

## ETH

**Ethereum** follows **BTC**, but with higher beta. When risk-on sentiment is high, **ETH** outperforms. When it crashes, **ETH** bleeds harder. The current **Fear & Greed** reading of **68** suggests **ETH** is primed for a move.

The **ETH/BTC** pair is the better indicator here. If **ETH** holds its value against **BTC** during the **FOMC** drop, it signals strength in the altcoin market. If it dumps, it’s a sign of weak conviction.

Bias is **Bullish**. The tokenization narrative on **Hyperliquid** and other L2s is still gaining traction, providing a fundamental floor. But we are trading macro, not fundamentals right now. We are trading the liquidity event.

Key level: The **ETH** local support. If it breaks, the move to the downside is fast. Invalidating the call is a broad market sell-off triggered by the **Core PCE Price Index m/m** forecast of **0.3%**. If inflation is sticky, **ETH** will suffer alongside **BTC**.

## Calendar Risks

Two events could flip our bias overnight.

First, the **FOMC Press Conference**. Any hint of "higher for longer" or inflation concerns will kill the rally. This is the primary risk.

Second, the **Advance GDP q/q** forecast of **3.2%**. If this number comes in hot, it validates the Fed’s hold and strengthens the USD. A hot GDP print is bearish for crypto. If it misses, it’s bullish. The variance between **1.5%** and **3.2%** is huge. The market is pricing in the high end. A miss is a potential pump.

We do not trade the forecast. We trade the reaction to the actual print.
