## BTC — Directional Bias: Sideways

Bitcoin is locked in a defensive consolidation phase. Without macro news to tilt the supply/demand balance, BTC is likely to respect technical support and resistance levels with high fidelity. The bias is flat. We expect price to chop within a defined range, punishing over-leveraged longs on the upside and shorts on the downside.

**Key Levels:**
Watch the immediate support zone. A break below this level with volume could signal a flush into deeper liquidity. Conversely, resistance holds the ceiling. A break above requires volume confirmation, which is unlikely in a data-less week.

**Invalidation:**
The call is invalidated only if an unexpected geopolitical event or a major exchange hack occurs. In the absence of these, the range holds. Do not chase breakouts. They will be fakeouts. The smart money is accumulating in the lower quartile of the range and shorting the upper quartile.

## ETH — Directional Bias: Choppy

Ethereum is exhibiting higher beta than Bitcoin but is constrained by the same macro silence. The ETH/BTC pair is likely to remain range-bound, reflecting a lack of clear narrative dominance. While BTC sits still, ETH may see slightly more volatility due to lower liquidity and higher sensitivity to spot flow changes, but the direction remains undefined.

**Key Levels:**
Monitor the ETH/USD support level. A failure here often leads to a sharper correction than BTC due to leveraged long positions being more prevalent. Resistance is the target for scalpers, not swing traders.

**Invalidation:**
A sudden surge in DeFi TVL or a major protocol upgrade announcement could provide the needed catalyst. Without it, ETH will follow BTC’s lead but with wider swings. The bias remains choppy. Expect whipsaws.
