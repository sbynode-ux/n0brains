## The Setup

Last week’s macro data was a study in quiet stagnation. The **Core PCE Price Index** came in at **0.3%** month-over-month, matching forecasts but showing no acceleration. **Final GDP** held steady at **1.5%** quarter-over-quarter. There was no shock. There was no surprise. The market absorbed these prints with a shrug, pushing Bitcoin to **$83,947** and Ethereum to **$2,699.82** with less than **0.2%** movement.

This lack of volatility is more dangerous than a crash. When the market is quiet and sentiment is high, it means everyone is on the same side of the boat. The next move will be violent because there are no opposing forces to absorb it. The **Non-Farm Employment Change** forecast for next week is **89K**, a sharp drop from the previous **162K**. If the labor market is cooling faster than the data suggests, the "soft landing" narrative fractures. If it holds, the Fed stays patient, and the risk-on trade continues until it breaks.

## BTC

Bitcoin is trading in a compression zone. The bias is **Neutral** because the directional trigger hasn't fired. We are looking at a consolidation phase where **$84,000** acts as immediate resistance and **$80,000** as the psychological floor.

The conviction is low because funding rates are likely elevated despite the quiet price action. This suggests late longs are trying to catch a breakout that isn't happening. The key level to watch is **$85,000**. A clean break above this with volume invalidates the neutral stance and targets **$90,000**. However, a drop below **$82,000** on rising volume signals that the "greed" is fake. The invalidation of the current setup is a break below **$80,000**, which would confirm a broader macro reversal.

We do not trade the range. We trade the break. Until then, we sit on our hands.

## ETH

Ethereum is currently decoupling slightly from Bitcoin's quiet momentum, up **0.20%** in the last 24 hours against BTC's **0.07%**. This minor outperformance is noise, not signal. The bias for ETH is also **Neutral**, tied tightly to BTC's liquidity.

ETH has less room to run if BTC stalls. If BTC consolidates, ETH tends to bleed slowly against it as traders rotate into safer assets or cash. The key level for ETH is **$2,750**. A break above this confirms strength. A break below **$2,600** exposes the leveraged longs in the DeFi sector. The invalidation of the ETH neutral stance is a sustained move above **$2,800** on high volume, which would signal a sector-wide rotation.

ETH is a beta play right now. It doesn't have its own narrative until the macro picture clarifies. We wait for BTC to make a decision first.
