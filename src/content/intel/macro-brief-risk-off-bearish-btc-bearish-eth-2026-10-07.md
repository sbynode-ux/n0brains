There were no high-impact USD data releases in the past seven days. The silence from the macro feed was deafening, yet the market decided to sell anyway. This absence of new information forced participants to look forward, and they looked at the **FOMC Meeting Minutes** scheduled for **Thursday, October 7th at 18:00 UTC** with extreme suspicion.

When sentiment sits at **74** (Greed) without a catalyst, the only direction left is down. Traders are rotating out of leverage-heavy positions ahead of a document that could reveal sticky inflation concerns or a harder stance on rate cuts. The lack of fresh data means the market is trading on fear of the unknown rather than reaction to the known. This is dangerous. It means any slight hint of hawkishness in the minutes will trigger a cascade of long liquidations.

The **Fear & Greed Index** has hovered between **65** and **74** all week, creating a false sense of security. The price action tells a different story. BTC dropped **2.78%** and ETH fell **4.91%** in the last 24 hours. The disconnect between sentiment and price is the signal. The market is greedy because it hasn't been hit yet, not because it is confident.

## BTC — directional bias, conviction, key levels to watch, what invalidates the call

Bitcoin is trading at **$83,732**, down **2.78%** in 24 hours. The bias is bearish.

The conviction is moderate-high because the move is happening on low volume, suggesting passive selling rather than active panic. However, the trend is broken. Bitcoin is failing to hold previous support levels, indicating that buyers are exhausted.

**Key Levels:**
- **Resistance:** **$86,000** is now the immediate ceiling. Any bounce here is a sell opportunity.
- **Support:** **$82,000** is the first line of defense. If this breaks, **$80,000** is the psychological floor.
- **Invalidation:** A daily close above **$86,500** would invalidate the bearish setup and suggest the recent drop was a bull trap. Until then, shorts are the only logical play.

The market is positioning for a drop. The liquidity pools are stacked below current prices, and with the FOMC minutes approaching, leveraged longs are exposed. We expect a sweep of these lows before any meaningful recovery.

## ETH — same structure as BTC

Ethereum is trading at **$2,579.97**, down **4.91%** in 24 hours. The bias is bearish, with lower conviction than BTC due to its higher beta and reliance on broader risk appetite.

ETH is underperforming BTC, which is a classic sign of risk-off sentiment. When traders get scared, they exit altcoins and stablecoins first, then ETH, and finally BTC. The fact that ETH is dropping nearly twice as fast as BTC signals that leverage in the ETH market is even more fragile.

**Key Levels:**
- **Resistance:** **$2,700** is the immediate hurdle.
- **Support:** **$2,500** is the critical level. A break below opens the door to **$2,400**.
- **Invalidation:** A reclaim of **$2,750** would suggest the sell-off is overblown.

The tokenized equity market on Hyperliquid is likely feeling this pressure too. As ETH drops, the liquidation maps for tokenized stocks and indices become more volatile. Traders holding leveraged positions in HIP-3 assets need to watch ETH closely, as it acts as the canary in the coal mine for the entire risk asset class.

## Calendar Risks — top 2-3 upcoming events that could flip the call

The only high-impact event this week is the **FOMC Meeting Minutes** on **Thursday**.

This is not a rate decision. It is a transcript of the debate. It reveals the internal disagreements among Fed officials. If the minutes show a split where even one member argues for maintaining higher rates for longer, the market will panic. This is the primary risk factor.

There are no other USD high-impact events scheduled this week. This emptiness amplifies the impact of the minutes. With no other distractions, every word in that document will be dissected for clues about the next rate move.

A dovish surprise — where the minutes suggest inflation is cooling faster than expected — could flip the bias to neutral or slightly bullish. But given the current price action, the market is pricing in a hawkish read. We should expect the baseline to remain bearish until proven otherwise.
