The market walked into a **Federal Reserve** rate decision, absorbed a **3.75%** hold, and hardly budged. BTC stuck **$64,000**. The **Fear & Greed Index** lodged itself in **Fear** for seven straight sessions. This is not a market that is cracking. This is a market compressing for the next move.

## The Setup

The **FOMC** delivered exactly what was priced: a hold at **3.75%** and a statement that keeps optionality alive. No hawkish surprise means the macro anchor held.

The focus now shifts to **July 30** — the day the macro calendar dumps its two highest-impact prints. **Advance GDP** for Q2 is forecast at **2.1%** , modestly hotter than the prior **2.0%**. **Core PCE** is the real needle: expected to soften from **0.3%** m/m to **0.2%** m/m.

This is the Goldilocks corridor. An economy that is not overheating but an inflation measure that is cooling. If the prints land inside expectations, the case for higher risk assets into the next Fed meeting gets automatic approval.

## BTC

Bias: **Bullish**. Conviction: **6/10**.

Why? Because the market has been tested by a macro event and signaled strength. Holding **$64,000** through the FOMC press conference is a minor win.

The immediate upside target is **$66,000**. A break of that level on a soft PCE print would likely trigger a cascade of short covering. Liquidity is low — the acceleration can be violent.

The invalidation level is **$62,000**. A daily close below this line suggests the compression resolved to the downside. That would put the range low at **$60,000** in play. Until that happens, we favor the upside.

## ETH

Bias: **Neutral**. Conviction: **4/10**.

ETH is the laggard everyone is watching. Sitting at **$1,915**, it needs a catalyst to reclaim **$2,000** and build momentum. The structure is weak: every bounce is sold.

The only bullish case for ETH right now is beta to BTC. If Bitcoin rips, ETH will get dragged along, but it will not lead the charge. A drop below **$1,850** would confirm the downtrend is intact and target the **$1,700** range low.

We are not short, but we are not allocating fresh capital here. Wait for structure to improve or a rotation signal.

## Calendar Risks

The entire week hinges on the **July 30** data dump.

**Core PCE m/m (July 30):** This is the single highest-impact event. A **0.1%** print is a rocket under risk assets. A **0.3%** or higher print reverses the Goldilocks narrative immediately.

**Advance GDP q/q (July 30):** A miss below **1.5%** introduces stagflation risk. A beat above **2.5%** questions the pace of rate cuts.

**FOMC Fallout Analysis:** The market will spend the rest of the week decoding the **FOMC** transcript and dot plot. Any concrete hint of a **September cut** will front-run the data.
