## The Setup
Seven empty days on the macro calendar. That's the story.

When high-impact USD data is absent, crypto trades on its own flows — and its own flows were selling. Sentiment bottomed at **25 (Extreme Fear)** mid-week, recovered to **29**, then slid back to 28. Grinding fear, not panic. That distinction matters: panic capitulates and resets positioning; grinding fear just thins it out.

The spot board confirms the tone. **BTC** at **$62,695**, down **0.70%** in 24h. ETH at **$1,843.80**, down **1.25%**. ETH bleeding twice as fast as BTC is the signature of a risk-off tape, not a healthy pullback.

Now the window flips open. **ISM Manufacturing PMI** lands Monday, and Friday stacks **Non-Farm Employment Change**, **Average Hourly Earnings**, and the **Unemployment Rate** into one binary print. A week of no data, then everything at once. That's how chop becomes a move.

## BTC
Bearish bias, moderate conviction.

BTC has spent the week above **$62,000** without any conviction either way. Support is the current bid at $62K, then the round **$60,000** line. Below that, $58K is where the next real bids sit. Resistance at **$65,000**, then $67K. We expect the grind lower to continue into Friday because positioning hasn't flushed — extreme fear readings on sentiment indices frequently arrive before the actual low.

What invalidates the call: a close above **$65,000** before Friday, which would signal absorption we're not seeing. Also a genuinely catastrophic NFP miss — think sub-**40K** — which can flip the tape from "growth scare" to "Fed put" in a single tick as rate-cut odds spike. That's the short-squeeze risk on an already-defensive book.

## ETH
Bearish bias, higher conviction than BTC.

ETH at **$1,843.80** is staring at the **$1,800** line. Below that, the air is thin down to $1,750 and **$1,680**. Resistance is **$1,920** to $1,950. In fear regimes, ETH doesn't just underperform — it leads the downside, because leveraged longs on high-beta collateral get liquidated first. The 24h damage, **1.25%** against BTC's **0.70%**, is exactly that mechanism at work.

What invalidates the call: ETH reclaiming **$1,920** while BTC holds $62K. That would mean risk appetite is genuinely returning, not just a BTC-specific bid. A lopsided BTC rally on a weak ETH typically marks the end of a move, not the start of one.

## Calendar Risks
**ISM Manufacturing PMI** (Monday, Aug 3) is forecast at **54.0** vs **53.3** prior — solidly expansionary. A print below **50** flips the growth narrative mid-week and sends BTC to test $60K. A beat above 55 reinforces the "no cuts soon" read, which is also bad for crypto. There is no good outcome in a hot ISM.

**Non-Farm Employment Change** (Friday, Aug 7) is the binary: forecast **88K** vs **57K** prior. Eighty-eight thousand is a weak number by historical standards — the forecast itself concedes the labor market is cooling. A sub-**50K** print ignites recession pricing and drags crypto down with everything else before any Fed-put thinking can start. A **150K+** beat reprices hawkish and does the same. The relief path is a print right around forecast: slowdown confirmed, collapse absent.

**Average Hourly Earnings m/m** is the sleeper. Forecast **0.3%**, prior **0.3%**. A **0.4%** or higher wage number spooks the bond market first, and crypto catches the spillover. A **0.2%** print is the only genuinely dovish setup on the calendar this week.
