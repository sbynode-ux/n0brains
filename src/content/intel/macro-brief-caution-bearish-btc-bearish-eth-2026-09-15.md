## The Setup

Traders are buying the rumor, but the chart is selling the truth. For the past seven days, sentiment has hovered in the **Greed** zone, ranging from **56** to **69**. This is complacency. When everyone expects the Fed to cut rates by **25 basis points** to **4.00%**, the market stops looking for upside and starts looking for exits.

We saw no high-impact USD data in the past week to disrupt this narrative, which allowed retail leverage to build up unchecked. Now, the **FOMC** meets on **September 16**. The consensus is baked in. The **Federal Funds Rate** is expected to drop to **fc=4.00%** from **3.75%**. The **Economic Projections** and **FOMC Statement** drop at **18:00 UTC**, followed by the press conference at **18:30 UTC**.

This is the classic "buy the rumor, sell the news" trap. The risk is not that the Fed cuts. The risk is that the cut is priced in, and the dot plot or language disappoints. When expectations are fully met, the only direction left is down.

## BTC — Bearish

Bitcoin is trading in a tight range above **$77,000**, but the volume is drying up. This is not accumulation. It is waiting. The bias is bearish because the leverage is long.

If **BTC** holds **$77,054**, it is merely consolidating before the event. If it breaks below **$76,500**, the liquidation cascade begins. We expect a sharp wick down into the **FOMC** release, followed by a potential V-shaped recovery if the cut is standard, or a slow bleed if the language is hawkish.

The key level to watch is **$77,000**. A break below this level invalidates the short-term bullish bias. The invalidation of the bearish call would require a sustained hold above **$78,500** on high volume post-fomc, which is unlikely given the current sentiment extremes.

## ETH — Bearish

Ethereum is following **BTC** down, but with slightly more weakness. At **$2484.04**, **ETH** is testing support. The **ETH/BTC** pair is showing relative strength, but that is a trap. It is not strength; it is just less pain.

The bearish case for **ETH** is stronger than for **BTC** because the tokenized equity market on Hyperliquid is sensitive to macro rates. If the Fed cuts, the yield on stablecoins drops, reducing the incentive to hold **ETH** for yield. The bias is bearish into the event.

Watch **$2,500** as immediate resistance. A failure to break this level during the FOMC press conference confirms the bearish structure. The invalidation of the bearish call would require **ETH** to break **$2,550** with conviction, which would require a massive shift in risk appetite that the current data does not support.

## Calendar Risks

The **FOMC Rate Decision** on **September 16** is the primary risk. The market is pricing in a cut, but the **dot plot** could signal fewer cuts in 2027. This would be a hawkish surprise.

The **FOMC Statement** language is the second risk. If the Fed emphasizes "data dependency" over "insurance," the market will sell.

The **Press Conference** is the third risk. Powell’s tone will dictate the volatility. A cautious tone will extend the bearish bias. A dovish tone might trigger a short squeeze, but only for a few hours.
