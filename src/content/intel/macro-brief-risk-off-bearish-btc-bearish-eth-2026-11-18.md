## The Setup

The last week of macro data was not kind to risk assets. On **November 17**, **Retail Sales m/m** came in at **0.1%** against a forecast of **0.1%**, but the headline figure of **0.8%** previous print suggests momentum is stalling. More importantly, **Core Retail Sales m/m** landed at **0.4%** against a forecast of **0.4%**, while the previous reading of **0.9%** indicates a sharp deceleration in real consumer spending.

This data reinforces the narrative that the US economy is cooling, but not collapsing. The Fed has room to hold rates steady, but they are also unwilling to cut aggressively into disinflationary territory without more proof. When the economy cools but inflation remains sticky, risk assets like crypto suffer the most.

On **November 16**, **Fed Chairman Warsh** spoke, offering no major surprises but reinforcing the hawkish undertones. His comments provided no relief for longs. Instead, they confirmed that the Fed is watching labor and price data closely before making any moves.

The market is now bracing for the **FOMC Meeting Minutes**, scheduled for **November 18** at **19:00 UTC**. These minutes will reveal the internal debate among Fed officials. Will they express concern about economic slowdown? Or will they worry about persistent core inflation? The current price action suggests the market expects the latter — or at least, a refusal to commit to early cuts.
