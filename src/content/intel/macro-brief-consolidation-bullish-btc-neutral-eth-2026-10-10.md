There is no new information to drive directional conviction. We are in a consolidation phase. The lack of scheduled high-impact USD events for the week ahead creates a vacuum. In these vacuums, volume dries up, spreads widen, and fake-outs become the primary mechanic for liquidity extraction.

## ETH — same structure as BTC

We expect **ETH** to remain range-bound between **$2,400** and **$2,600**. There is no catalyst to drive **ETH** out of this range. The lack of scheduled events means no new liquidity is entering the **ETH** ecosystem. Traders are rotating into **BTC** for safety, leaving **ETH** stagnant.

## Calendar Risks

There are no high-impact USD events scheduled for the week ahead. This is both a risk and an opportunity. The risk is that any unexpected data leak or geopolitical headline can trigger a sudden, volatile move without warning. The opportunity is that the lack of scheduled events allows for cleaner technical analysis.

The top risk is a surprise data print. If **CPI** or **PPI** data were to leak or be released early, it would break the consolidation. We are watching for any headlines related to inflation. If inflation data comes in hotter than expected, **BTC** could drop sharply. If it comes in cooler, **BTC** could rally. The market is waiting for a catalyst.
