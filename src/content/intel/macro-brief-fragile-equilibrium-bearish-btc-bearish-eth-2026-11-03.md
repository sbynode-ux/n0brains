The Fear and Greed Index has spent five of the last seven days below 35, signaling a market that is aggressively pricing in downside risk while waiting for a catalyst.

That catalyst is coming. The macro silence of the past week is not peace. It is the calm before the **Non-Farm Employment Change** print on **November 6**, where forecasts call for **65K** jobs against a weak **29K** prior. ## The Setup

For the past seven days, the macro calendar has been empty. No CPI, no PCE, no Fed speakers. This vacuum allows sentiment to drift unchecked. The result is a slow bleed in confidence. Fear and Greed moved from **37** down to **30**, then ticked slightly up to **51** on Friday, but that neutral reading is misleading. It reflects a pause, not strength.

The market is currently trading on momentum and positioning, not fundamentals. With no high-impact USD releases to anchor price discovery, liquidity is thin. This is the most dangerous environment for leverage. When the next data drop hits, the lack of recent reference points means algorithms and traders will react based on stale assumptions. The disconnect between the **51** neutral sentiment and the **30** Fear readings from earlier in the week shows a market that is fundamentally weak but temporarily resting. We expect this rest to end violently.

## BTC

Bitcoin is facing a bearish bias. The primary driver is the upcoming labor data. A print of **65K** jobs, especially if accompanied by a rise in the Unemployment Rate above the forecasted **4.2%**, would signal cooling demand. In the current macro regime, cooling demand is not "soft landing" good news. It is "rate cut delayed" or "liquidity tightening" bad news for risk assets.

The key level to watch is the recent consolidation range low. If BTC breaks below this support on high volume during the **13:30 UTC** release, the path of least resistance is down. The invalidation of the bearish call is a miss on the employment side combined with a significant drop in Average Hourly Earnings, which would fuel stagflation fears and force the Fed's hand toward aggressive easing. However, the probability favors the bear case. The crowd is already positioning for downside, as evidenced by the sustained Fear index. We trade the consensus until it breaks.

## ETH

Ethereum follows Bitcoin but with higher beta. The same macro pressures apply, but ETH suffers from additional structural headwinds. The tokenized equity market on Hyperliquid, which n0brains covers extensively, often leads ETH's move during macro events. If BTC stumbles, ETH usually drops 1.5x to 2x harder.

The bias remains bearish. The risk-off sentiment in crypto is amplified by the broader stock market's sensitivity to labor data. Any weakness in the US dollar or equity markets during the **Congressional Elections** aftermath will hurt ETH's correlation plays. The key level is the ETH/BTC pair support. If ETH/BTC breaks, it signals a liquidity crunch in the altcoin segment, dragging ETH down independently of BTC's moves. We expect ETH to underperform BTC in the short term as traders flee to the relative safety of the leading asset.

## Calendar Risks

Three events dominate the week ahead, and any one of them can flip the bias.

First, the **Congressional Elections** result on **November 3**. This is not a data release, but a political binary event. Markets hate uncertainty. If the outcome suggests a divided government or policy shifts that impact regulation or fiscal spending, volatility will spike immediately. We expect knee-jerk reactions that last 24 hours.

Second, the **Non-Farm Employment Change** on **November 6**. This is the main event. The forecast of **65K** jobs is already elevated. A miss could trigger a massive rally in risk assets if interpreted as a forced Fed pivot. A beat could crush them if interpreted as persistent inflation. The **Unemployment Rate** forecast of **4.2%** is the secondary check. Any deviation here confirms the trend.

Third, the **Average Hourly Earnings m/m** print. At a forecast of **0.3%**, this number drives the inflation narrative. If wages rise faster than expected, the "higher for longer" rate narrative returns, and BTC/ETH sell off. This is the most likely bearish trigger.
