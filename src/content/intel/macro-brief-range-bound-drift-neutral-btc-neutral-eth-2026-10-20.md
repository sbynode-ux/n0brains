## BTC

Bitcoin is stuck in a consolidation phase. The bias is neutral, with a slight lean toward downside if support breaks, but no conviction either way. We expect chop. The key level to watch is the current range high. A break above it with volume could trigger a short squeeze, but without a macro catalyst, that move will likely stall.

The invalidation of the neutral call is a close below the local support zone. If BTC loses that level, the "Greed" sentiment will flip to Fear rapidly, triggering long liquidations. We are watching for volume dry-up. If volume declines while price stays flat, a break is imminent. The direction is less important than the timing. Trade the break, not the range.

## ETH

Ethereum is lagging BTC. The ETH/BTC pair has been grinding lower, reflecting weak relative performance. The bias is bearish on the pair, meaning ETH underperforms BTC in this environment. However, in USD terms, ETH is also range-bound.

The key level here is the liquidity zone below the current support. If BTC breaks down, ETH will likely break down harder due to lower liquidity. The invalidation of the bearish pair view is a strong BTC rally. If BTC pumps, ETH might catch up, but the momentum is currently with Bitcoin. We do not recommend chasing ETH longs in this regime. The risk/reward favors shorts or waiting for a clear breakout.

We are entering a week with no scheduled high-impact USD events. This is a double-edged sword. On one hand, there is no unexpected data to crash the market. On the other hand, there is no catalyst to drive a sustained trend. The risk is low-volatility decay.

The primary risk is not data, but sentiment fatigue. The Fear & Greed index has been high for several days. If buyers run out of steam, the market could drift lower simply due to lack of interest. We are monitoring social sentiment for signs of exhaustion. If the "Greed" index stays above **60** for more than two more days without a price breakout, a reversal is likely.
