The market has voted with its feet, and it is running for the exits.

Crypto sentiment has collapsed from **50** (Neutral) to **26** (Extreme Fear) in just seven days. This isn't a correction. It is a structural retreat of risk capital. Bitcoin is bleeding below **$75,206**, and Ethereum is taking a harder hit at **$2,145.55**. The liquidity that fueled the recent rallies has evaporated, leaving retail traders exposed and leverage positions ripe for liquidation.

## The Setup — what released and what didn't, why it matters

Nothing happened yesterday. No CPI. No PPI. No Fed speeches. The silence is deafening because the market is holding its breath for the next two weeks.

When high-impact US data is absent, volatility compresses. But compression is not stability. It is energy storage. The recent drop in Fear & Greed indices — hitting **25** on Monday before ticking slightly back to **26** — signals that traders are no longer buying the dips. They are waiting for the other shoe to drop.

The setup is simple. We are in a post-event lull, heading directly into a gauntlet of inflation data. The market is pricing in a worst-case scenario: sticky inflation forcing the Fed to delay rate cuts, or worse, hint at hikes. The absence of data is not a green light. It is a vacuum waiting to be filled by macro reality.

## BTC — directional bias, conviction, key levels to watch, what invalidates the call

**Bias: Bearish.**

Bitcoin is failing to hold support. The drop to **$75,206** is significant. It breaks the psychological barrier of **$75k** and exposes the market to further downside. The conviction is high because the volume is drying up. Buyers are absent. Sellers are aggressive.

**Key Levels:**
- **Support:** **$74,000**. If this breaks, we see **$72,000**.
- **Resistance:** **$76,500**. A reclaim here is unlikely without a catalyst.

**Invalidation:** A hard close above **$77,000** on high volume would signal a trap for bears. Until then, the path of least resistance is down. The macro backdrop — sticky inflation expectations — favors the dollar and hurts risk assets.

## ETH — same structure as BTC

**Bias: Bearish.**

Ethereum is underperforming Bitcoin. The **-3.87%** drop in 24 hours is a clear signal of weakness. ETH/BTC pair is trending lower, indicating that capital is rotating out of altcoins and into stablecoins or out of crypto entirely.

**Key Levels:**
- **Support:** **$2,100**. A break below this opens the door to **$2,000**.
- **Resistance:** **$2,200**. Unlikely to hold without a broader market rally.

**Invalidation:** A breakout above **$2,250** would suggest a short-term relief rally. However, the structural trend remains down. The narrative for ETH is weak without a bullish catalyst for DeFi or Layer 2 adoption.

## Calendar Risks — top 2-3 upcoming events that could flip the call

Two weeks of data. Two weeks of volatility.

**1. Core CPI m/m (Nov 10)**
Forecast: **0.2%**. Previous: **0.3%**.
If the print comes in hotter than **0.2%**, expect a sharp sell-off. The market is pricing in cooling inflation. Any surprise upward revision will shatter that illusion.

**2. CPI m/m (Nov 10)**
Forecast: **0.3%**. Previous: **0.6%**.
A broader measure. A miss here confirms the cooling trend. A beat confirms the sticky narrative. Either way, expect volatility.

**3. Core PPI m/m (Nov 13)**
Forecast: **0.3%**. Previous: **0.7%**.
Wholesale inflation. If producers are still raising prices, consumer inflation will follow. This is the second wave of risk.

These are not minor events. They are regime-defining. A hot CPI print could trigger a cascade of long liquidations. A cold print could spark a short squeeze. But given the current fear, the bias leans toward the downside if data disappoints.
