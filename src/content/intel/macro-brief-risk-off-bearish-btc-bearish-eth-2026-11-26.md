The market is not waiting for permission to fall further. **Extreme Fear** has persisted for seven consecutive days, with the index stuck between **11** and **13**, signaling that liquidity is retreating faster than buyers can accumulate.

Bitcoin sits at **$60,658** and Ethereum at **$1,698.96**, both posting modest **3.5%** daily gains that feel like dead cat bounces rather than reversals. The regime is clear: risk-off. ## The Setup — what released and what didn't, why it matters

The **Core PCE Price Index** is the Federal Reserve’s preferred inflation gauge. A hold at **0.3%** month-over-month suggests price pressures are contained, but it does not mean they are gone. More importantly, it confirms the Fed is in no rush to cut rates aggressively. The "higher for longer" narrative remains intact because inflation is sticky, not because it is exploding.

Simultaneously, the **Prelim GDP** print of **3.5%** indicates economic growth is slowing but still positive. This creates a toxic mix for risk assets: growth is decelerating (bad for earnings) while inflation is persistent (bad for liquidity).

The critical takeaway is what *didn't* happen. Normally, a GDP beat triggers a risk-on rally. Here, the market shrugged it off. This divergence tells us that macro fundamentals are secondary to technical overextension. Traders are not buying the good news because they are waiting for the bad news. The absence of high-impact USD events for the rest of this calendar week removes any immediate catalyst for a trend change. Without a shock, the path of least resistance is down.

## BTC — directional bias, conviction, key levels to watch, what invalidates the call

Bitcoin is currently trading in a consolidation box between **$60,000** and **$62,000**. The daily close above **$60k** is psychological, not structural. The conviction is **Bearish** because the volume profile shows weak hands taking profits on the daily rise.

Key levels to watch:
- **Support**: **$58,500**. If this breaks, we enter uncharted territory where stop-losses cluster.
- **Resistance**: **$63,200**. A daily close above this level would suggest the bears are exhausted. Until then, any rally is a sell opportunity.
- **Invalidation**: A sustained move above **$65,000** with increasing volume. This would signal institutional accumulation, but there is zero evidence of it currently.

The **Fear & Greed** index at **11** is a contrarian indicator for bottom-fishing, but it is also a trend indicator for capitulation. When fear is this extreme, it usually precedes a final shakeout before a true bottom. We are likely in the middle of that shakeout.

## ETH — directional bias, conviction, key levels to watch, what invalidates the call

Ethereum is underperforming Bitcoin, trading at **$1,698.96**. The ETH/BTC pair is weakening, which historically leads to absolute declines in ETH. The conviction is **Bearish** because Ethereum lacks the narrative tailwinds that Bitcoin enjoys during macro uncertainty.

Key levels to watch:
- **Support**: **$1,650**. This is the last line of defense for short-term longs.
- **Resistance**: **$1,750**. Breaking this requires a breakout in BTC first.
- **Invalidation**: A decisive break above **$1,800** on high volume. This would suggest ETF inflows are restarting, but current data shows stagnant flows.

The lack of catalysts for Ethereum is stark. While Bitcoin benefits from its "digital gold" narrative, Ethereum is still viewed as a tech asset, and tech is selling off. The **3.7%** daily gain is noise. The trend is down.

## Calendar Risks — top 2-3 upcoming events that could flip the call

Any hint of hawkishness in those minutes could trigger a cascade of liquidations. Additionally, watch for unexpected geopolitical headlines or exchange outflows, which often drive price action in the absence of macro data.
