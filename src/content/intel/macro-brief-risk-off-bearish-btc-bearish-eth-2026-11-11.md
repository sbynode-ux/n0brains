## The Setup

The data dropped on **2026-11-10** at **13:30 UTC** was supposed to be the catalyst. The **Core CPI m/m** came in at **0.2%** against a forecast of **0.2%**, matching the previous month’s **0.3%**. The headline **CPI m/m** printed at **0.3%**, a significant deceleration from the prior **0.6%**.

On paper, this looks like a victory for disinflation. In practice, it was a non-event. The market didn’t rally. It didn’t crash. It just drifted lower. Why? Because **0.3%** headline CPI is still sticky. The Fed doesn’t care about last month’s deceleration; they care about the trajectory. If core inflation is stuck at **0.2-0.3%**, the path to rate cuts is blocked. The "good news is bad news" trade is broken. Good news (lower inflation) is not good enough to trigger a relief rally because the absolute level remains elevated.

We saw the same pattern in **Fear & Greed**. The index hit **25** on **2026-11-06** and has struggled to break above **32** since. That is not accumulation. That is capitulation without follow-through. Smart money is not buying the dip; they are waiting for the next liquidity sweep.

The key level to watch is **$72,500**. If we lose that, we open a gap down to **$70,000**. That is the psychological floor. If **$70,000** breaks, we are looking at a retest of lower supports. Liquidity is thin on the upside. The long side is crowded with retail traders hoping for a reversal. Short sellers are waiting for that hope to die. We advise against chasing longs here. The risk-reward is terrible.

## ETH

Ethereum is underperforming Bitcoin. At **$2,055.51**, down **1.74%** in 24 hours, ETH is showing weakness. The ETH/BTC pair is drifting lower, which is a classic risk-off signal. When altcoins bleed faster than Bitcoin, it means capital is fleeing to safety or stablecoins.

The bias for ETH is bearish. The support at **$2,000** is critical. If we break that, the next stop is **$1,850**. The invalidation is a move back above **$2,200** on high volume. Without a catalyst, ETH will continue to lag. DeFi TVL is stagnant, and there is no new narrative driving demand.

We see no reason to be long ETH here. The macro tailwinds are absent. The technicals are weak. The sentiment is fearful. It is a recipe for further downside.
