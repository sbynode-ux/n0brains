The market is pricing in a soft landing with a **64** Fear & Greed score, but the silence before the **Core CPI** print on **October 14** is exactly where liquidity gets hunted.

We are in a regime of complacent greed. Sentiment has hovered between **57** and **69** for the past week, suggesting traders are comfortable with risk. That comfort is dangerous. When sentiment is this uniformly bullish without a fresh catalyst, the market becomes fragile to any surprise in inflation data. We don't guess. We grade the conditions before the trade.

## The Setup

The macro calendar is about to wake up. For the past seven days, the USD high-impact feed was empty, leaving price action to drift. That drift is now ending. The entire week’s directional bias hinges on the **Core CPI** and **Core PPI** releases.

The consensus for **Core CPI** is **0.2%** month-over-month and **2.5%** year-over-year, down from **0.3%** and **2.4%** respectively. This implies a continued cooling of inflation. However, the **CPI** headline is expected to rise to **0.6%** m/m from **0.4%**, driven likely by energy or housing lags.

The bigger risk is **Retail Sales**. The forecast calls for a sharp deceleration from **1.2%** to **0.3%** m/m. If consumers are still spending despite high rates, the Fed has less room to cut. If they have stopped, the economy is slowing faster than priced in.

We don't trade the headline. We trade the deviation. A beat on CPI or Retail Sales would invalidate the current bullish drift. A miss would fuel the risk-on rally. Until the data drops, we are in a holding pattern.

## BTC — Bullish

Bitcoin is showing relative strength. The Fear & Greed index is at **64**, indicating greed, but BTC has not yet extended its recent moves aggressively. This suggests accumulation rather than euphoria.

**Bias:** Bullish.
**Conviction:** Medium.

The path of least resistance is higher, but only if inflation data comes in line or below expectations. A hot CPI print would trigger a liquidation cascade in leveraged long positions.

**Key Levels:**
- **Support:** Watch the previous consolidation zone. A break below signals a rejection of the higher lows.
- **Resistance:** The recent highs. A break above confirms the continuation of the uptrend.

**Invalidation:** A **Core CPI** print above **0.3%** m/m or **Core PPI** above **0.3%** m/m would flip the bias to bearish in the short term. This would signal sticky inflation, forcing the market to repricer rate cut expectations.

## ETH — Neutral

Ethereum is lagging Bitcoin. While BTC is breaking out, ETH is consolidating. This divergence is common in the early stages of a macro-driven rally. Traders are rotating into the higher-beta asset (BTC) first.

**Bias:** Neutral.
**Conviction:** Low.

ETH needs BTC to hold its ground. If BTC pulls back, ETH will likely underperform due to its lower liquidity and higher correlation to DeFi activity, which is currently muted.

**Key Levels:**
- **Support:** The 20-day moving average. A break below suggests the outperformance trade is over.
- **Resistance:** The recent consolidation range high. A break above confirms ETH is ready to catch up.

**Invalidation:** If BTC breaks support, ETH will likely see a sharper decline. Conversely, if BTC rallies hard, ETH may not follow immediately, leaving it vulnerable to funding rate squeezes.

## Calendar Risks

Three events could flip the current bias.

1.  **Core CPI (October 14, 12:30 UTC):** The most critical data point. A miss on the forecast of **0.2%** m/m would be bullish. A beat would be a immediate risk-off trigger.
2.  **Core PPI (October 15, 12:30 UTC):** This measures wholesale inflation. A hot print would suggest the CPI cooling is temporary, undermining the bullish case.
3.  **Fed Chairman Warsh Speaks (October 16, 03:30 UTC):** Any hint of hawkishness or delay in rate cuts would crush risk assets. Watch for keywords like "data dependent" or "persistent inflation."
