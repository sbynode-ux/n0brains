The US labor market is tightening, and risk assets are paying the bill. With the S&P 500 hitting new highs on the back of AI euphoria, the underlying labor data tells a different story—one of cooling momentum that threatens to snap the bull market’s spine. We are in a **Risk-Off** regime. The evidence is in the fear.

## The Setup

The market is currently trapped in a divergence. Equities are pricing in a soft landing, but crypto sentiment has reverted to **Fear** (index: **30**). This disconnect is dangerous. Over the past week, sentiment has hovered between **26** and **37**, signaling that traders are not buying dips with conviction. They are waiting for a catalyst to exit.

Last week, the **Congressional Elections** passed without immediate market chaos, but the silence is deceptive. The real catalyst arrives today. The **November 6 Non-Farm Payrolls** release is the most important macro event in the current cycle. The forecast calls for **65K** jobs, a significant jump from the previous month’s **29K**. The market expects a resumption of labor demand. If the data comes in hot, the Fed’s rate cut expectations collapse. If it comes in cold, recession fears take over. Both outcomes are bad for leverage.

We are not neutral. We are bearish. The liquidity that fueled the Q3 rally is drying up. Spot BTC is down **5.32%** in the last 24 hours, and ETH is down **7.39%**. This is not a correction; it is a repricing of risk.

## BTC — Bearish

Bitcoin is trading at **$76,048**, a level that has failed to hold multiple times this month. The bias is **Bearish** with high conviction for the near term. The primary driver is the USD strength anticipated from today’s NFP data. A strong jobs report strengthens the dollar, which directly suppresses BTC.

Key levels to watch are the **$72,000** support zone and the **$78,500** resistance. The current price is sandwiched between these two. A break below **$72,000** triggers a cascade of long liquidations. A break above **$78,500** would require a massive surge in volume and a dovish Fed pivot, neither of which is priced in.

The invalidation of the bearish call is simple: a miss on NFP. If jobs come in below **40K**, the recession trade activates, and BTC could rally as a hedge. However, the probability favors a hot print or a neutral print that disappoints bulls. The liquidation maps show heavy short interest below **$70,000**, but the upside liquidity is thin. We do not want to catch a falling knife.

## ETH — Bearish

Ethereum is underperforming Bitcoin, down **7.39%** to **$2,240.3**. The bias is **Bearish**. ETH’s beta to BTC is amplified during risk-off regimes. When traders deleverage, they sell the highest-beta assets first. ETH is that asset right now.

The key levels are **$2,100** support and **$2,350** resistance. The current price is testing the lower bound of the recent consolidation range. A break below **$2,100** opens the door to **$1,900**. The tokenized equity markets on Hyperliquid often move in tandem with ETH, and the liquidation heat maps there show significant short positioning that could fuel a squeeze if BTC stabilizes. But with BTC weak, ETH is exposed.

The invalidation of the bearish call is a strong NFP miss combined with a spike in DeFi TVL. If capital flows back into ETH staking or L2s, it provides a floor. Currently, TVL is flat. There is no bid.

## Calendar Risks

The next **7 days** are critical. The **November 6 Non-Farm Payrolls** at **13:30 UTC** is the primary risk. The forecast is **65K** jobs, but the previous print was only **29K**. The variance is huge. A surprise hot print could trigger a **10%** drop in BTC within minutes. A surprise cold print could trigger a **5%** rally, but likely only a dead cat bounce.

Secondary risk is the **Average Hourly Earnings m/m** release, forecast at **0.3%**. Wage growth is the Fed’s primary metric for inflation. If earnings come in at **0.4%** or higher, the Fed’s hands are tied. This is the single most bearish scenario for crypto. It removes the rate cut narrative entirely.
