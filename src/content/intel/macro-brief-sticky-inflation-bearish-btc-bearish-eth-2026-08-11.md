## BTC

Directionally, we're bearish. The bias is **moderate conviction**, not high — this is an event-driven call, not a structural one. The key level to watch is **$62,000**. The bias is **bearish with higher conviction than BTC**. Why? Because ETH has no narrative hedge. BTC has "digital gold" chatter; ETH has nothing but yield compression and token unlocks. A hot CPI print hits ETH harder, and a soft print doesn't help it proportionally because the market is already short the ratio. Invalidation for the ETH call is a **core CPI miss to the downside (0.1% or less)** combined with a **break above $1,950** — that would signal the market is looking through inflation to growth. We don't think that happens.
