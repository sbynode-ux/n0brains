While the headline number hit **3.6%** (down from **3.4%**), the monthly acceleration to **0.6%** (up from **0.4%**) suggests sticky underlying pressures. More importantly, the **Core CPI** beat its forecast of **2.5%** relative to the previous **2.4%**. The market interpreted this as "good news" because it was lower than expected, but for a Fed still tethered to a **2%** target, any uptick in core services is a lagging indicator of pain.

We are moving from a "data-dependent" week to a "headline-sensitive" week.
