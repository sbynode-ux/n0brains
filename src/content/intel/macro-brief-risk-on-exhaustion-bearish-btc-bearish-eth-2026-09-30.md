The lack of volatility allows leveraged longs to pile in without fear of immediate liquidation. This is dangerous. When the **Core PCE Price Index** drops on **2026-09-30**, the vacuum will be shattered.

## BTC — Directional Bias: Bearish

Bitcoin is showing clear signs of exhaustion. The price is consolidating below key resistance, and volume is drying up. The **Fear & Greed** index at **71** suggests overextension. Historically, when sentiment hits this level without a corresponding breakout in price, a correction follows.

**Conviction:** High. We expect a test of lower supports.

**Key Levels:**
- **Resistance:** **$85,000**. A close above this level is required to invalidate the bearish bias. Until then, every rally is a sell opportunity.
- **Support:** **$82,000**. A break here opens the door to **$80,000** and potentially lower.
- **Invalidation:** A daily close above **$86,500** with strong volume would signal renewed institutional accumulation, flipping the bias to neutral-bullish.

The risk/reward is skewed against longs. Leverage is building on the long side, and when the **Core PCE** data arrives, any miss on the inflation front will trigger a cascade of liquidations. We do not trade hope; we trade probabilities. The probability points down.
