The **Core CPI** came in at **2.5%**, comfortably below the feared spike, and the market didn't just shrug it off — it charged. **Bitcoin** is trading at **$82,188** with a **1.51%** daily gain, while **Ethereum** is ripping higher at **$2,465.02**, up **4.20%** in the same window. The regime is clear: liquidity is returning, inflation is tamed, and the only question left is how far the greedy run can stretch before hitting a wall.

## The Setup

The data dump on **October 14** was the catalyst. The **Core CPI y/y** landed at **2.5%** against a forecast of **2.5%** and a prior of **2.4%**. The headline **CPI y/y** hit **3.6%** vs **3.4%**, but the market ignored the headline noise and focused on the core deceleration. This print validated the narrative that the Federal Reserve is already winning the fight, or at least that the worst of the sticky services inflation is behind us.

The follow-through on **October 15** confirmed the bid. **Core Retail Sales** grew **0.6%** month-over-month, beating the **0.3%** forecast and reversing the prior month’s **1.4%** drop. **Core PPI** rose **0.3%**, slower than the **0.6%** expected. These numbers scream disinflationary momentum in the real economy. When wages and prices slow down without a recessionary collapse, central banks are forced to stay dovish. That is the fuel for risk assets.

Sentiment mirrors this confidence. The **Crypto Fear & Greed Index** has hovered between **53** and **69** over the last seven days, averaging in the **Greed** zone. Traders are no longer defensive. They are positioning for upside, not hedging against crash. The absence of a panic sell-off on the **3.6%** headline CPI print proves that the market has priced in the "higher for longer" fear and is now trading the "softer landing" reality.

## BTC — Directional Bias

**Bitcoin** is holding the **$82,000** level with conviction. The daily close above **$82,000** is critical. If we lose **$80,000**, the bullish structure breaks, and we likely revisit **$78,000** to fill liquidity. However, the current momentum suggests we are in a consolidation phase before the next leg up.

The bias is **Bullish**. The thesis is simple: lower inflation expectations reduce the discount rate for future cash flows, benefiting hard assets. With the **Core CPI** cooling, the path of least resistance for **BTC** is higher, provided the **Fed** does not surprise with hawkish commentary.

Key levels to watch:
- Support: **$80,000** (psychological and recent breakout level)
- Resistance: **$85,000** (previous high and liquidity pool)

Invalidation occurs if **BTC** closes daily below **$79,500**. This would signal that the CPI print was a "sell the news" event and that underlying demand is weak. Until then, dips are buys.

## ETH — Directional Bias

**Ethereum** is outperforming **Bitcoin** right now. The **4.20%** daily gain versus **1.51%** for **BTC** shows strong relative strength. This is typical in risk-on environments where leverage flows into higher-beta assets. **ETH** is currently at **$2,465.02**, and the momentum is strong.

The bias is **Bullish**. The rally in **ETH** is driven by two factors: general crypto market beta and specific ecosystem news. The tokenized equity narrative on **Hyperliquid** is gaining traction, and **ETH** is the primary settlement layer for these derivatives. As more capital flows into tokenized assets, **ETH** demand increases.

Key levels to watch:
- Support: **$2,400** (local consolidation zone)
- Resistance: **$2,550** (next psychological barrier)

Invalidation occurs if **ETH** loses **$2,350**. This would indicate a rotation back into **BTC** or a broader market correction. However, the current volume profile suggests buyers are stepping in aggressively at lower levels.

## Calendar Risks

The macro calendar for the week ahead is **empty** of USD high-impact events. This is unusual and dangerous. An empty calendar often leads to low liquidity, which can cause exaggerated moves in either direction. Traders should be cautious of fakeouts.

The next major risk is the **Fed Chairman Warsh Speech** scheduled for **October 16**. While we are in the covered window, the market is waiting for his tone. If he hints at further cuts, **BTC** and **ETH** could surge. If he sounds hawkish, the rally could stall. The empty calendar means there is no data to anchor prices, so the speech will be the sole driver of volatility.

Watch for:
- **Fed Chairman Warsh Speech** (October 16)
- Any unexpected geopolitical headlines
- Large **BTC** or **ETH** whale movements
