## The Setup

The evidence is in the prints from last week, and they are ugly. On **November 10**, the **Core CPI m/m** came in at **0.2%**, matching forecasts, but the headline **CPI m/m** held firm at **0.3%** against a previous **0.6%**. That headline decline looked like progress until you looked deeper. The underlying services inflation remained sticky, and the market’s reaction was immediate and brutal.

Then came **November 13**. The **Core PPI m/m** printed at **0.3%**, exactly in line, but the headline **PPI m/m** surged to **0.4%** from a previous **1.3%**. Wait, that’s a drop. Let’s look closer. The previous month was **1.3%**, so **0.4%** is better, but the *Core* PPI holding at **0.3%** while headline drops suggests base effects are masking persistent core inflation. More importantly, the market didn’t see "good news." It saw confirmation that price pressures are embedded in the supply chain.

The Federal Reserve has explicitly stated they will not cut rates until they see sustained progress on inflation. These prints do not show sustained progress. They show a plateau. This means the liquidity tailwinds that fueled the 2026 altcoin season are evaporating. We are moving into a regime where cash is king, and leverage is suicide.
