## The Setup

The past seven days were quiet on the macro front. No US high-impact data dropped. The absence of noise is the signal. When the calendar is empty and price action consolidates near highs, the market is digesting the previous leg up.

Sentiment data confirms this digestion is complete. The index moved from **59** to **73** in one week. That is a rapid shift from "Greed" to "Extreme Greed" territory. Historically, this compression precedes a volatility expansion. The market is coiling.

The next release breaks the coil. **Core CPI m/m** is scheduled for **October 14** at **12:30 UTC**. The forecast is **0.2%**, down from **0.3%**. If inflation cools, the Fed puts remain cut. If it surprises hot, the liquidity narrative fractures. This single print will dictate whether the **$83k** level holds as support or becomes a liquidity trap.

## BTC — directional bias, conviction, key levels to watch, what invalidates the call

Bitcoin is bullish with high conviction. The bias is toward **$88,000** by month-end, provided the CPI print does not trigger a hard re-rate of rate cut expectations.

The key level is **$83,000**. This is the current support floor. A close below **$81,500** on a 4-hour chart would signal distribution, not a dip buy. The invalidation point is a CPI print above **0.4% m/m** combined with a strong USD reaction. That combination would force a liquidation cascade targeting **$78,000**.

Positioning is tight. The lack of recent volatility suggests leverage is low. A short squeeze upside is more probable than a long liquidation downside, unless the macro data shocks. The trend is your friend until the Fed speaks.

## ETH — same structure as BTC

Ethereum is bullish, but with a caveat. ETH traded at **$2,553.38** yesterday, up **2.20%** in 24 hours. It is outperforming BTC on momentum but lagging on dominance.

The bias is toward **$2,800** if BTC holds **$83k**. ETH’s beta is higher. A **1%** move in BTC results in a **2-3%** move in ETH. The key level is **$2,500**. A break below this opens the door to **$2,350**.

## Calendar Risks

Three events could flip the call.

First, **Core CPI m/m** on **October 14**. This is the primary risk. A miss to the upside (**>0.3%**) is the bear case trigger.

Second, **Core Retail Sales m/m** on **October 15**. Forecast is **0.5%**. Strong retail data supports the "soft landing" narrative, which is bullish for risk assets. Weak data is ambiguous.

Third, **Fed Chairman Warsh Speaks** on **October 16**. Tone matters. Hawkish language on inflation persistence will cap upside regardless of data.

Do not trade the rumor. Trade the reaction.
