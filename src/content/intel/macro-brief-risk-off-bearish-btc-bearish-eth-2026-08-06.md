## The Setup

If we miss, we get a different kind of chaos: stagflation panic. Either way, volatility is the only certainty.

## BTC

**Bias: Bearish.** Conviction: Medium-high. The path of least resistance is down into the **NFP** print.

We are watching **BTC** attempt to hold a critical support zone. A break and close below the **$84,000** level would open the floodgates to the **$78,000** range, where the liquidation cascades live. The funding rate is negative, but that is a consequence of fear, not a contrarian buy signal. It can stay negative while price bleeds.

The **Unemployment Rate** is forecast to hold at **4.2%**. If that ticks higher *and* payrolls miss, we get a "bad news is good" bounce. That is the only scenario that invalidates our bearish bias in the short term. If we get a hot jobs number, that bounce is the one to sell. The structure is broken. We are not buyers until we see a daily close above **$92,000**.

## ETH

**Bias: Bearish.** Conviction: High. **ETH** is structurally weaker than BTC. It has been lagging on every single bounce, and the volatility is steeper on the downside.

The **ETH/BTC** pair is bleeding, which tells you where the smart money is parking. We expect **ETH** to underperform any BTC relief rally and to lead the downside in a risk-off flush. The critical level to watch is **$2,900**. A break of that with volume signals the next leg down toward the **$2,600** support.

The only thing that saves ETH is a massive hawkish surprise from the Fed that causes a "risk-on" rotation, but that narrative is not in the cards for this week. The **Average Hourly Earnings** figure is the key for ETH. If **0.3%** m/m holds, sticky inflation is confirmed, and the high-beta asset gets sold first.

## Calendar Risks

The **NFP** print on **August 7th** is the catalyst. It is the only thing that matters.

**1. The "Hot" Print (>100K):** Hawkish shock. Yields rip. BTC dumps. ETH dumps faster. This is the base case.
**2. The "Cold" Miss (<50K):** The market will initially cheer. We expect a dead-cat bounce. Use it to short. A miss is still a "stay the course" for the Fed, which is bearish for liquidity.

**Average Hourly Earnings** **0.3%** is the secondary risk. If we see a **0.4%** or higher, it confirms the sticky inflation narrative. That is the real liquidity killer.
